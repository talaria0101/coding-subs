Grok Pricing and Plans 2026: Every Subscription Tier and API Cost

Grok is free to start. The paid Grok subscriptions are SuperGrok Lite at $10 a month, SuperGrok at $30, SuperGrok Plus at $100 and SuperGrok Heavy at $300, with annual billing at $100, $300, $1,000 and $3,000 a year. SuperGrok Business costs $30 per seat per month. The xAI API is billed separately per token, from $1.00 per million input tokens for Grok Build 0.1 to $2.00 input and $6.00 output for Grok 4.6 (grok.com and docs.x.ai, September 2026). X Premium+ ($40 a month on X) also includes SuperGrok access.

This guide lists every Grok plan xAI sells as of September 15, 2026: what each subscription costs per month and per year, what it includes, how the weekly usage pool works, how X Premium fits in, and what the API charges per model. xAI, which now publishes as SpaceXAI, changed its lineup this summer (SuperGrok Lite and SuperGrok Plus are new, and older API models were retired), so figures from earlier in 2026 are out of date. Every price here is taken from xAI's and X's own pages, linked in the sources at the end. For what each model actually does, see our companion guide, Grok models explained.

TL;DR: Free: $0, includes Grok 4.6 with limits. SuperGrok Lite: $10/month. SuperGrok: $30/month (adds Grok Bot). SuperGrok Plus: $100/month (1080p video, priority access). SuperGrok Heavy: $300/month (largest agent team, X Premium+ included). Annual billing saves about 17% on every tier. Business: $30 per seat. API: Grok 4.6 and 4.5 at $2 input and $6 output per 1M tokens, Grok 4.3 and 4.20 at $1.25 and $2.50, Grok Build 0.1 at $1 and $2, all doubled once a prompt reaches 200K tokens. No Grok plan adds folders or full-text search to your chat history; AI Toolbox (formerly ChatGPT Toolbox) does, with a free plan.

How Grok Usage Limits Work on Paid Plans

Paid Grok plans share one weekly usage pool across Grok products such as Chat, Imagine, Voice and Build, instead of separate daily limits per product. xAI's Grok FAQ says this system rolled out in June 2026. A chat message uses little of the pool; a high-quality video or a long coding task uses much more.

  • Where to check it: Settings, then Usage, on web or mobile. It shows a percentage used, a breakdown by product, your weekly reset time and any credit balance.
  • When you hit the limit: paid features pause until the weekly reset, but the free tier limits on Chat and Voice still work because they reset on their own schedule.
  • Extra Usage Credits: sold on the web only, from $5, used after your weekly allowance runs out, and they expire one year after purchase. xAI says credits cost more per action than the usage included in a plan.
  • Auto Top Up: adds credits automatically when your balance is low, up to a monthly cap you set.

xAI's advice is direct: if you regularly exceed your weekly pool, a higher plan is better value than repeatedly buying credits. xAI does not publish the size of each plan's pool in tokens or messages, only the relative wording on its plan cards ("2x longer conversations" on Lite, "5x" on SuperGrok, "significantly higher usage" on Plus).

Grok API Pricing per Model

The xAI API has no subscription: you pay per million tokens, and rates depend on the model and on whether a prompt crosses 200K tokens. These are xAI's published text model rates from docs.x.ai/developers/pricing (last updated September 7, 2026), in USD per 1M tokens.

grok-4.5500K$2.00$0.30$6.00$4.00 / $0.60 / $12.00
grok-4.31M$1.25$0.20$2.50$2.50 / $0.40 / $5.00

Once a prompt reaches the 200K threshold, xAI bills every token in that request at the long context rate, not only the tokens above 200K. Worked from the published rates: a request with 100K input tokens and 5K output tokens costs about $0.23 on grok-4.6 and about $0.14 on grok-4.3, while a 250K-token prompt with the same output costs about $1.06 on grok-4.6. xAI's Grok 4.6 announcement also mentions a fast variant at twice the price.

  • Retired models: grok-4-0709, grok-4-1-fast, grok-4-fast, grok-3 and grok-code-fast-1 were retired on May 15, 2026. Requests to those names are redirected and billed at the replacement model's price, grok-4.3 ($1.25 / $2.50) for most and grok-build-0.1 for grok-code-fast-1.
  • Batch API: 20% off for grok-4.3 and the three grok-4.20 models, usually completed within 24 hours. grok-4.6, grok-4.5 and grok-build-0.1 do not accept batch requests.
  • Priority Processing: 2x standard rates on every token type, billed only when the response confirms the priority tier.
  • Server-side tools: $5 per 1,000 calls for Web Search, X Search and Code Execution, $2.50 for Collections Search and $10 for attachment search, plus the tokens used. From September 21, 2026, X Search changes to $5 per 1,000 posts fetched and $10 per 1,000 user profiles fetched.
  • Imagine and Voice: grok-imagine-image-2.0 output starts at $0.04 per image, grok-imagine-video-1.5 costs $0.08, $0.14 or $0.25 per second at 480p, 720p or 1080p, speech to speech is $0.08 per minute, text to speech $15 per 1M characters, and speech to text $0.10 per hour (batch) or $0.20 per hour (streaming).

To estimate a workload before you run it, the token counter and AI cost calculator help turn text length into token counts. xAI's FAQ states that API credits are non-refundable.

Which Grok Plan Should You Choose?

Start on Free, because it already includes Grok 4.6, and upgrade only when a specific limit gets in your way. The tier that fits depends on what you run into first.

  • Students and occasional users: Free covers everyday questions. SuperGrok Lite at $10 a month is the step up when you want Expert mode or keep hitting the conversation length.
  • Writers, analysts and researchers using Grok daily: SuperGrok at $30 a month, or $300 a year, gives 5x longer conversations, faster replies and Grok Bot for background jobs.
  • Video creators and marketers: SuperGrok Plus at $100 is the first individual tier with 1080p video and priority access at peak times.
  • People who already pay for X Premium+: check whether your $40 X subscription covers you before buying SuperGrok, since X lists SuperGrok access for Premium+.
  • Engineers running long agent tasks: SuperGrok Heavy at $300 for the largest agent team and highest usage, or the API if the work runs in your own code.
  • Teams of two or more: SuperGrok Business at $30 per seat adds shared billing, seat management and exclusion from training by default.
  • Developers on a budget: grok-4.3 at $1.25 / $2.50 with the 20% batch discount, or grok-build-0.1 for coding.

How to Cancel SuperGrok or Request a Refund

Cancel where you subscribed: grok.com under Settings, Billing for web purchases, Apple's Subscriptions settings for the iOS app, and Google Play for Android. xAI's FAQ adds that if the Manage Subscription button does not open on the web, an ad blocker or another extension may be blocking it, so try an incognito window.

  • Web and Google Play refunds are reviewed by xAI through its refund request form; approved refunds typically reach the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days.
  • App Store refunds are handled by Apple.
  • X Premium refunds are handled by X, which says subscriptions are non-refundable unless required by law.

If you are leaving Grok altogether, save the conversations you need first. Our guide on backing up Grok conversations covers the options.

Limitations and What xAI's Own Plans Do Well

xAI's pricing is unusually transparent on the API side and deliberately vague on consumer usage. The API page publishes exact per-token, cached, long context, batch and tool rates, and the Grok FAQ explains cancellations, refunds and the weekly pool clearly. What xAI does not publish is how large each plan's weekly pool is, so "5x longer conversations" cannot be converted into a message count, and nobody outside xAI can tell you exactly when a SuperGrok user will hit the limit.

Two more caveats. Prices here are the USD figures shown on September 15, 2026; X states that Premium uses local pricing, and taxes can apply. And xAI has changed its lineup often in 2026, adding Lite and Plus and retiring older API models, so check grok.com/plans before you commit to an annual plan. How much is Grok per month?
