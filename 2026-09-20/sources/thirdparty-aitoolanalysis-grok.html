🆕 What Changed in 2026 (New $10 Lite Tier + Grok 4.3)

If you read our original guide in January, three things have moved. First, xAI split the middle of the ladder: SuperGrok Lite launched at $10/month on March 25, 2026, giving light users Grok Imagine and one AI agent without paying the full $30. Second, the model got faster and cheaper to run: Grok 4.3 arrived on April 30 as the new flagship with a one-million-token context window, and it is rolling out across the paid tiers in stages, with Grok 4.5 already in private beta at Tesla and SpaceX. Third, and least happily, Grok’s image generation moved fully behind a paywall in March and became the subject of lawsuits and government investigations that we cover in detail below.

The good news for anyone comparing the SuperGrok subscription price to last year: the two anchor numbers most people care about — $30 for standalone SuperGrok and $300 for Heavy — did not change. What changed is everything around them.

⚖️ SuperGrok vs X Premium+: Which Should You Choose?

This is still the most common question about Grok pricing. Both plans give you the same Grok 4 model access; the $10 difference buys X platform features, not more AI. We keep a dedicated head-to-head in our X Premium+ vs SuperGrok comparison, but here is the short version.

SuperGrok ($30/month) includes:

  • Full access to Grok 4, with Grok 4.3 rolling out in stages
  • DeepSearch (live web + X research mode)
  • Big Brain / Think mode (step-by-step reasoning)
  • Unlimited Grok Imagine image generation (soft fair-use caps)
  • Voice Mode for hands-free conversations
  • Extended 128K-token context memory
  • Available standalone at grok.com — no X account needed

X Premium+ ($40/month) adds:

  • Everything on the Grok side, PLUS the X platform perks below
  • Blue verification checkmark
  • Ad-free (or largely ad-reduced) X browsing
  • Creator monetization eligibility and larger revenue share
  • Highest reply-visibility boost and longer post limits

The decision is simple: if you actively use X, Premium+ folds Grok and your platform perks into one bill. If you only want the AI, pick SuperGrok and keep the $120/year. Stacking both is rarely worth it.

🆓 Free Grok Limits: What You Actually Get Now

Before paying any SuperGrok subscription price, know what free gets you — and what it no longer does. The free tier tightened in 2026.

  • Text prompts: roughly 10 requests every 2 hours on the lighter Grok 4 Mini model
  • Image & video generation: removed — xAI pulled the last free Grok Imagine access on March 19, 2026, so any creation now requires a paid plan
  • Model access: Grok 4 Mini, not the full Grok 4 or 4.3
  • DeepSearch & Voice: limited or excluded
  • Cost: $0, no credit card required

Free is now best treated as an evaluation lane for text chat. If image generation is why you were looking at Grok, the cheapest door is SuperGrok Lite at $10. For research-heavy free use, Perplexity AI still offers a more generous free tier, and Google Antigravity provides free frontier-model access for coding.

🐣 SuperGrok Lite ($10/month): The New Entry Tier

The biggest structural change to the SuperGrok subscription price in 2026 is the arrival of SuperGrok Lite at $10/month, announced by Elon Musk on March 25. It sits between the free tier and full SuperGrok, and it exists mainly to give light users Grok Imagine access without the $30 commitment.

  • Basic Grok Imagine image and video generation at 480p resolution and 6-second clip length
  • Roughly 2x longer Grok chats than the free tier
  • Access to one AI agent
  • Daily creation caps apply — this is not a full-power plan

💡 Key Takeaway: Pick Lite if you mostly want quick image generation and slightly longer chats. The moment the 480p ceiling, 6-second clips, or daily caps start to bite, step up to full SuperGrok — the extra $20 removes almost every wall Lite puts in front of you.

🚀 Is the SuperGrok Subscription Price ($30) Worth It?

The standard $30/month plan is the one most readers end up comparing. It is the cheapest standalone route to the full Grok experience, and for daily AI users it is where the SuperGrok subscription price earns its keep.

Grok 4 access (Grok 4.3 rolling out): the flagship reasoning model, at a 128K context window on this tier. Grok 4.3 adds native video input and native document output and is being pushed to standard SuperGrok in stages after its April 30 launch.

DeepSearch: Grok’s research mode, comparable to Perplexity’s research feature — it searches across the live web and X, then returns a cited synthesis. Big Brain / Think mode exposes step-by-step reasoning for harder problems. Unlimited image generation through Grok Imagine (with soft fair-use limits), and Voice mode for spoken chat, round out the tier.

SuperGrok is worth $30/month if:

  • You use an AI assistant daily for work or study
  • DeepSearch and real-time X awareness matter to your work
  • You generate images regularly and can accept the safety trade-offs below
  • You do not want or need X platform features

Skip SuperGrok if:

  • The free or $10 Lite tier already covers your usage
  • You also want X Premium+ perks (get Premium+ instead)
  • You primarily need coding help — Claude Code or GitHub Copilot serve that better
  • The image-generation safety controversy is a dealbreaker for you

🔍 REALITY CHECK

Marketing Claim: “Unlimited AI access with the world’s smartest model.”

Actual Experience: “Unlimited” image generation has soft caps — heavy users report throttling after 50–100 rapid generations, and reset windows are inconsistent. On raw capability, Grok 4 is genuinely strong on math and real-time data, but Claude still leads on coding benchmarks and ChatGPT on ecosystem depth.

✅ Verdict: Real value at $30 for daily AI users who want live X data and image generation — just temper the “unlimited” and “smartest” marketing.

⚡ SuperGrok Heavy ($300/month): Is It Worth It?

At $300/month, SuperGrok Heavy is one of the most expensive consumer AI subscriptions on the market. It is also the only consumer tier with confirmed full Grok 4.3 access today, plus Grok 4 Heavy on top.

Grok 4 Heavy: a multi-agent reasoning system with a 256K-token context window that runs parallel test-time compute. It posts the highest benchmark scores in xAI’s lineup — 100% on AIME 2025 and 50.7% on Humanity’s Last Exam were the numbers that first put it on the map. Full Grok 4.3 lands here first, and you also get the maximum rate limits everywhere, priority access during peak load, and early previews of new xAI products.

🔍 REALITY CHECK

Marketing Claim: “The world’s most powerful AI, for $300/month.”

Actual Experience: Grok 4 Heavy is genuinely impressive on benchmarks, but $300 is 15x ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro. Unless you have specific high-value workflows — quant research, intensive document analysis, heavy agentic pipelines — the ROI is hard to justify for a single user.

✅ Verdict: Worth it only for professionals and teams where a better answer directly pays for itself. For everyone else, standard SuperGrok at $30 is the smarter buy.

⚠️ The Grok Safety Crisis: Where It Stands in 2026

Any honest guide to the SuperGrok subscription price has to cover this, because it is the single biggest reason to hesitate. What began as a December 2025 image-generation scandal has, through 2026, hardened into active litigation and government action.

⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING

What happened: From late December 2025, Grok’s image tools were exploited to generate non-consensual sexual deepfakes and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). xAI has said it began investigating on December 28, 2025.

Where it stands in mid-2026: California ordered xAI to stop the sexualized-minor outputs in January. Formal investigations are open in the EU (Digital Services Act), the UK (Ofcom, Online Safety Act), and Canada (Privacy Commissioner, PIPEDA). US House Energy & Commerce Democrats opened an inquiry, and a class-action lawsuit was filed in March on behalf of three teenagers. Reporting indicates xAI has set aside a large legal reserve while conceding it has not found a reliable technical fix.

⚠️ Our take: Text features are a separate question, but if image or video generation is your reason to subscribe, understand that you are paying into the exact product surface at the center of these cases. Every major competitor moderated its way around this; xAI monetized it.

Notably, the $30 SuperGrok and $40 X Premium+ tiers include an R-rated “Spicy Mode” for Grok Imagine — the adult-content feature that sits at the heart of the controversy. That business decision is why the safety score in our comparison sits so low. For the full picture on the model and the fallout, see our Grok review. This is a genuinely sensitive area; if you are weighing it for reasons beyond a purchase decision, treat the underlying issue with the seriousness it deserves.

📝 How to Subscribe to SuperGrok (and Save)

For standalone SuperGrok or SuperGrok Lite, subscribe directly on the plans page rather than through X:

  1. Visit the official plans page at grok.com/plans.
  2. Select SuperGrok Lite ($10), SuperGrok ($30), or SuperGrok Heavy ($300).
  3. Create an account or sign in, then enter payment (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal).
  4. Use Grok at grok.com or via the mobile apps.

For X Premium or Premium+, subscribe inside the X app under Settings → Premium. To lower the SuperGrok subscription price, pay annually: SuperGrok at $300/year works out to about $25/month, and students with a verified .edu address have historically been able to access SuperGrok free in the US — confirm current eligibility on the plans page, since these promotions change.

❓ SuperGrok Pricing FAQs

Q: How much does SuperGrok cost in 2026?

A: The SuperGrok subscription price is $30/month or $300/year for the standard plan. SuperGrok Lite is $10/month and SuperGrok Heavy is $300/month. Inside X, Premium is $8 and Premium+ (which also includes Grok) is $40.

Q: What is SuperGrok Lite and is it worth $10?

A: SuperGrok Lite is the entry tier launched March 25, 2026. For $10/month you get basic Grok Imagine (480p, 6-second clips), longer chats than free, and one AI agent. It is worth it if you want occasional image generation; upgrade to full SuperGrok once the caps frustrate you.

Q: Is SuperGrok worth it versus ChatGPT Plus?

A: SuperGrok is $30 versus ChatGPT Plus at $20. The extra $10 buys real-time X data, cheap image and video generation, and Grok’s voice. For general work, ChatGPT or Claude offer better value; for live social-media awareness, Grok is unique.

Q: What is the difference between SuperGrok and X Premium+?

A: SuperGrok ($30) is Grok AI only. X Premium+ ($40) bundles the same Grok with X perks like ad-free browsing, the checkmark, and creator monetization. Choose SuperGrok if you do not actively use X.

Q: Is there still a free version of Grok?

A: Yes, for text: about 10 prompts every 2 hours on Grok 4 Mini. But free image and video generation was removed on March 19, 2026, so any Grok Imagine use now needs at least the $10 Lite tier.

Q: Is SuperGrok Heavy worth $300/month?

A: Only for professionals with high-value workflows. Grok 4 Heavy tops xAI’s benchmarks and Heavy is the only tier with full Grok 4.3 today, but for most users standard SuperGrok at $30 is more than enough.

Q: Should the safety controversy affect my decision?

A: If you subscribe mainly for image or video generation, yes — that is the feature under active legal and regulatory scrutiny in 2026. Text-only use is a separate question, but the controversy is a legitimate reason to prefer a competitor with a stronger safety record.

On pure capability-per-dollar for real-time data and image generation, it holds up. What drags the overall score down is not the price — it is the unresolved safety crisis around the exact features you would pay for.
