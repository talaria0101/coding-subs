Claude Code Weekly Limits Are Now 25% Higher Permanently: Anthropic Confirms the September 14 Change

By AI Catchup Editorial·Published August 29, 2026·Updated September 14, 2026·8 min read
Claude CodeAnthropicUsage LimitsPricingDeveloper ToolsAI Coding Tools

As of September 14, 2026, Anthropic documents the permanent increase on its own support article: weekly limits in Claude Code are 25% higher than they were before the May-September promotion for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. The 50% promotion ended September 13 at 11:59 PM PT. Five-hour limits, prices, and other Claude products are unchanged.

Anthropic has permanently raised standard weekly limits in Claude Code by 25% over their pre-promotion level for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans, effective September 14, 2026. The change is now on an Anthropic-owned page: the Help Center article that tracked the 50% promotion was rewritten on September 14, 2026 and says, "starting September 14, 2026, weekly limits in Claude Code are 25% higher than they were before the promotion for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans." The 50% promotion it replaces ended September 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. ClaudeDevs had announced both halves on August 29 (ClaudeDevs announcement).

Key takeaways

  • Effective September 14, 2026: standard Claude Code weekly limits are permanently 25% higher than before the promotion. Documented by Anthropic on September 14.
  • Named plans: Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise, in both the ClaudeDevs post and the support article.
  • What ended: the 50% promotion, which ran May 13 through September 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. The new level is about 17% below the promotional level and 25% above the pre-May baseline.
  • Scope: a Claude Code weekly-limit change. Neither source announces a change to five-hour limits, plan pricing, or Claude and Cowork limits.
  • What to do: run /usage in the CLI and compare the weekly figure with what you saw in August.

What Anthropic's support article says

The article's opening paragraph, as of September 14, 2026:

This promotion was a limited-time boost on top of your plan. It ran from May 13 through September 13, 2026. We were planning to return weekly limits to their original levels, but we know many of you found the extra usage helpful. So while the full promotional boost couldn't last, we're making part of it permanent: starting September 14, 2026, weekly limits in Claude Code are 25% higher than they were before the promotion for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. We have a lot more in the works around usage, visibility, and control, so stay tuned.

The rest of the article is now written in the past tense: the promotion "was 50% higher", "applied to Claude Code only, everywhere you use it: the CLI, IDE extensions, desktop, and the web", did not affect five-hour limits, and excluded Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats. Its title has not changed and still reads "Claude Code May-August 2026 weekly limits promotion". (Anthropic Help Center)

What ClaudeDevs announced on August 29

The announcement is short but specific:

Starting September 14, we're permanently raising standard weekly limits in Claude Code by 25% for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. Until then, the current 50% increase will be in place.

The follow-up says the new level works out to a 17% reduction compared with the temporary level available at the time of posting. Both halves have now happened as stated: the 50% level ran through September 13, and the 25% level took over on September 14. (ClaudeDevs follow-up)

Neither the post nor the support article gives absolute message counts or token quantities. That is expected: Anthropic's general guidance says Claude Code usage depends on the plan, model, feature, and length and complexity of the work rather than a fixed message count. (Claude pricing)

Who is included

Both sources name the same four groups:

  • Pro
  • Max
  • Team
  • Seat-based Enterprise

The support article excluded Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats from the promotion by name and said no action was needed because the increase applied automatically, everywhere Claude Code runs: the CLI, IDE extensions, desktop, and the web. The one sentence about the permanent increase does not restate the exclusions, so treat Free and consumption-based Enterprise as not named rather than as confirmed either way. (Anthropic Help Center)

Claude Code is included in Anthropic's paid plans, and its usage shares a pool with other Claude activity where the plan documentation says so. Anthropic's pricing page says paid plans have rolling five-hour limits and weekly limits on top, with capacity depending on the model and features used. (Claude pricing)

What is not changing

The change is to standard weekly limits in Claude Code. Neither the ClaudeDevs post nor the September 14 support-article update says that Anthropic is:

  • changing Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise subscription prices;
  • changing the rolling five-hour usage limit;
  • increasing limits for Claude web, mobile, desktop, or Cowork;
  • publishing a fixed number of additional messages;
  • changing API token pricing or consumption-based billing; or
  • adding a new plan.

The support article closes with "We have a lot more in the works around usage, visibility, and control, so stay tuned." It gives no specifics, and this page does not speculate about them.

How to check your own account

Anthropic's support guidance says eligible users did not need to change account settings for the promotion and could run /usage in the CLI to view their limits. The same command shows the new level. (Anthropic Help Center)

For a clean comparison:

  1. Run /usage and note the weekly-limit display.
  2. Compare it with a reading from before September 14 if you have one; the weekly figure should be about 17% below the promotional level.
  3. Check that your plan is one of the four named groups.
  4. If the displayed limit conflicts with the support article, use Anthropic's support guidance rather than assuming a hidden account error.
  5. Keep five-hour and weekly limits separate when comparing results.

What the 17% note means

ClaudeDevs said the permanent 25% level is a 17% reduction compared with the 50% level. That arithmetic is consistent with comparing the two multipliers: 1.25 divided by 1.50 is about 0.833, so the new level is about 83% of the promotional level and therefore about 17% lower than it.

That does not mean weekly capacity has fallen below the pre-promotion baseline. Relative to that baseline, the new level is 25% higher; it is only lower than the temporary 50% promotion that ran from May 13 to September 13.

Bottom line

Claude Code's standard weekly limits are permanently 25% higher than they were before the promotion, from September 14, 2026, for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. Anthropic documents this on its own support article as of September 14, and the 50% promotion it replaces ended September 13 at 11:59 PM PT. Run /usage to see what your own plan shows. (ClaudeDevs announcement; Anthropic Help Center)
